Olist delivery reliability

Four ways into one analysis of 96,203 delivered Brazilian e-commerce orders, January 2017 to August 2018. Same data, same five questions, four different jobs: the full sweep, the argument, the deck, and the map.

Research objective

To understand how reliably Olist's delivery network performs as demand rises and shifts across the country, in order to decide what delivery standard Olist can commit to as it scales.
